Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to drive revenue for our White Water Rafting Tour company, Rafting Momentum. This website will also be an area where we can share the experience that our clients will receive when they book with us. This website will provide an easy way for clients to book trips with us. It will also be a place where we can share educational tips and personal opinons about White Water Rafting.

Audience

The target audience for our website and service is people in Colorado seeking a thrilling experience on the water. The preferred age of our clients is 12 years old and up, while you must be 18 to book with us. People who visit our site are those that don't have access to a raft, river guide and who don't know a good location for a fun, White Water excursion. Viewers will be able to access our website on desktop, laptop and mobile devices.
