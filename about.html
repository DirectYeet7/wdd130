About Me

Hello! I'm Derrick McConnell and I am currently a student at Brigham Young University–Idaho, pursuing a degree in Business Management. I am from Pocatello, Idaho and I have a passion for helping others, building meaningful connections, learning new skills, and making a positive impact in the lives of those around me.

My career goal is to become a funeral director, where I hope to combine my business education with my desire to serve individuals and families during some of life's most difficult moments. I believe that compassion, professionalism, and attention to detail are essential to providing meaningful service and support.

This portfolio showcases some of the hard skill that I have developed during my time at BYU-Idaho. Within these pages you will see my proficiencies in HTML along with CSS. I invite you to look through my projects and connect with my on LinkedIn to get to know more about me.
